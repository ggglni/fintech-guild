Membership is selective. We look for senior operators with a demonstrated record of impact on the fintech industry.

Admission Criteria
  • Seniority

    Substantial demonstrated experience at a senior level in fintech or DeFi.

  • Impact

    A record of high level impact on the fintech industry, not merely participation in it.

  • Operator Status

    Candidates must be preferably builders — people who have shipped and run products.

  • Contribution

    Proactive contribution to the community is expected. Passive membership is not accepted.

Evaluation Process
  • 01Admission runs on two intakes per year, on a semestral cycle.
  • 02All candidates submit a formal application form with supporting credentials.
  • 03Applications are reviewed privately and individually by Fintech Guild staff against the criteria above.
